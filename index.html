01 — OVERVIEW

The brief

What this workshop is, and why it's worth an afternoon.

You'll be placed on a small team with other first-years and handed a real, open-ended design constraint — the kind with no single correct answer. Over the course of the day, your team sketches concepts, argues about trade-offs, builds a low-fidelity prototype, and tests it in front of the room.

It's not a competition and nothing is graded. The point is to get comfortable with the engineering design process — define, ideate, prototype, test, iterate — before you hit it for real in your first-year design courses.

06 — QUESTIONS

FAQ

Do I need any design or engineering background?

No. This workshop is built for first-years — the whole point is a first honest run at the design process before your courses formalize it.

Can I sign up with friends?

You can request to be on the same team, though teams are capped at five to keep things workable.

What if I can't make the full four hours?

Try to stay for the whole session if you can — teams depend on every member for the build and the final presentation. Let us know ahead of time if you have a hard conflict.

Is this open to all engineering streams?

Open to all first-year engineering students, regardless of which program you're entering.
